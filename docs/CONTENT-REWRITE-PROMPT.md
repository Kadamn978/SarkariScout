# Content Rewrite Prompt — RozgarScout

Use this prompt whenever you need to rewrite, create, or review content pages (Terms, Privacy, About, FAQ, landing copy, error pages, etc.) for RozgarScout.

---

## The Prompt

Rewrite the following content so it sounds like a real person wrote it — not a chatbot, not a legal template generator, not a marketing team. Keep the original ideas, structure, and legal substance intact. Only change the phrasing and tone.

### What to keep
- All legal substance and key terms
- The original structure and section organization
- Specific numbers, timeframes, and technical details
- Links and contact information

### What to change
- Replace predictable phrases with natural ones
- Remove corporate filler: "We strive to", "industry-standard", "leverage", "utilize", "comprehensive", "robust", "seamless"
- Remove AI tells: "In today's digital landscape", "It's important to note", "Furthermore", "Moreover", "Additionally", "In conclusion"
- Remove unnecessary hedging where the point is already clear

### Tone rules
- **Short sentences mixed with longer ones.** Not everything needs to be a paragraph.
- **Direct and confident.** Say what you mean. Don't soften every statement.
- **Occasional contractions.** "Don't" instead of "do not" when it feels natural.
- **Real opinions where appropriate.** "We're good, but we're not infallible" > "We make every reasonable effort to ensure accuracy."
- **Conversational rhythm.** Vary sentence length. Avoid repetitive patterns like three bullet points starting the same way.
- **No unnecessary sophistication.** If a simple word works, use it. "Use" not "utilize." "Help" not "facilitate." "Get" not "obtain."
- **Match the content type:**
  - Legal pages (Terms, Privacy): Still formal where it matters, but readable. A normal person should be able to read the whole thing without zoning out.
  - About page: Conversational. Like you're explaining to a friend what you built and why.
  - FAQ: Direct answers. No preamble. Get to the point.

### What NOT to do
- Don't add new data or facts
- Don't invent testimonials, statistics, or fake user stories
- Don't pad with extra explanations — if the point is made, move on
- Don't use "we believe", "we are committed to", or "we aim to" unless it's genuinely the right phrase
- Don't make it longer. If anything, make it shorter.

### Examples of AI-sounding → human-sounding

| ❌ AI / Corporate | ✅ Human |
|---|---|
| "We strive to provide accurate and timely information" | "We work hard to keep things accurate" |
| "In compliance with applicable data protection laws" | "We follow India's DPDP Act" |
| "Industry-standard security measures" | "Encryption, hashed passwords, regular audits" |
| "Your use of the Platform constitutes acceptance" | "Keep using the site after changes go live and that means you're okay with them" |
| "We implement robust safeguards" | "We take this seriously" |
| "The Platform is provided on an as-is basis" | "We can't promise perfection" |
| "Please do not hesitate to contact us" | "Reach out to us" |
| "Furthermore, we would like to clarify" | Just say the thing directly |
| "It is important to note that" | Delete it. Just say the thing. |
| "We are committed to protecting your privacy" | "We don't sell your data" |
