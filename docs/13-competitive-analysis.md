## 3. Our Unique Advantages (What Nobody Else Has)

1. **AI-Powered Job Matching** — Profile-based eligibility matching. Others show ALL jobs; we show ONLY jobs you qualify for.
2. **Application Tracker** — Track applied → exam → admit card → result. Nobody offers this.
3. **Change Detection** — Auto-detect when exam date, venue, or deadline changes. Alert users instantly.
4. **Smart Deadline Reminders** — WhatsApp/SMS reminders with countdown. Not just email.
5. **Eligibility Pre-Check** — "Am I eligible?" instant checker before applying.
6. **Document Wallet** — Store photo, signature, certificates for quick form filling.
7. **Salary Comparison Tool** — Compare salaries across different government jobs.

| government jobs 2026 | High | Medium | /jobs |

| [exam name] notification 2026 | Medium | Low | /jobs/[id] |
| [exam name] admit card | Medium | Low | Admit card page |
| [exam name] result | Medium | Low | Result page |
| [state] government jobs | Medium | Low | /state/[name] |
| [qualification] govt jobs | Medium | Low | /qualifications/[level] |

### SEO Tactics:
1. **Job detail pages** with schema.org JobPosting markup
2. **State-specific landing pages** (e.g., "Maharashtra Government Jobs 2026")
3. **Qualification-specific pages** (e.g., "12th Pass Government Jobs")
4. **Exam-specific pages** with full details (notification, syllabus, pattern, results)
5. **Daily updated content** — Google loves fresh content
6. **Internal linking** — Link related jobs, exams, results
